Step-by-Step Usage Guide

Step 1: Set Up Your Real Estate Projects

Go to Real Estate Base > Projects > Projects and click New.

  • Type in your project name, site location, and total land area.
  • Assign a site engineer or project manager so they receive updates.
  • Enter your RERA registration number and attach regulatory approvals so everything stays compliant.

Step 2: Add Towers & Building Wings

Go to Real Estate Base > Projects > Buildings to break your project into individual structures.

  • Pick the main project, enter the wing name (such as Tower A or Block B), and specify the total number of floors.
  • Note down elevator details and common area spaces for future maintenance reference.

Step 3: Register Units & Set Prices

Go to Real Estate Base > Inventory > Units to add individual apartments, offices, or shops.

  • Enter the carpet area, floor number, bedroom count, and facing direction.
  • Choose if the unit is available for sale, lease, or both, and set the list price.
  • Watch the status tag change automatically as sales or leases are signed.

Step 4: Monitor Business Performance

Open Real Estate Base > Dashboard anytime for an instant operational summary.

  • Check summary cards to see total active projects, available units, and monthly revenues.
  • Click on any metric card to immediately open the full list of underlying records.
