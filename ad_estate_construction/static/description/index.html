Construction Costing & Site Procurement Manager

Control civil project budgets with clear work phases (WBS), bill of quantities (BOQ) cost estimates, and material requests connected directly to Odoo Purchase Orders.

Work Breakdown Phases Simple BOQ Estimator Site Material Requests One-Click Purchase Orders

Prevent Budget Overruns & Uncontrolled Site Spending

Construction projects quickly blow past their budgets when site engineers order materials without checking original estimate limits.

This module breaks building work down into clear stages (Foundation, Structure, Utilities, Finishes). Engineers create BOQ estimates with raw material needs, labor rates, and overhead margins.

Material Requisitions ensure site orders never exceed approved BOQ caps and automatically generate official Odoo Purchase Orders (`purchase.order`) upon manager approval.

How It Makes Everyday Work Easier

  • Structured Project Phases: Group BOQ lines by Foundation, Structural Frame, Utilities, and Finishing works.
  • Clear Costing Tool: Calculate subtotal costs, overhead percentages, profit margins, and final estimates easily.
  • Site Material Requests: Site supervisors request materials linked directly to approved BOQ lines.
  • Automatic Purchase Orders: Turn approved material requests into standard Odoo Purchase Orders with one click.

Step-by-Step Usage Guide

Step 1: Create a BOQ Budget Baseline

Go to Construction > Civil Planning > BOQs and click New.

  • Select your master Project and Building Wing.
  • Add material and labor line items categorized under work phases (Cement, Steel Rebar, Electrical Fitting).
  • Enter estimated unit rates, overhead %, and margin % to establish the total cost ceiling.
  • Click Confirm BOQ to lock in the baseline budget.

Step 2: Submit Material Requisitions

Go to Construction > Procurement > Material Requisitions.

  • Site engineers select the parent BOQ sheet and add requested material items.
  • The system automatically checks if requested quantities exceed remaining un-ordered BOQ limits.
  • Click Submit for Approval to forward the request to the construction manager.

Step 3: Generate Purchase Orders

Once the requisition is approved by the manager:

  • Click Create Purchase Order at the top of the form.
  • Pick the material supplier or vendor contact.
  • The system creates a standard draft Odoo Purchase Order automatically with quantities and vendor links attached.

Step 4: Track Usage & Delivery

Track materials through standard purchasing steps:

  • Confirming the Purchase Order updates consumed quantity figures on the BOQ sheet in real time.
  • Receive site deliveries through standard Odoo Inventory stock transfers.
  • Check purchase history anytime via the smart button on your BOQ sheet.

Getting Started in 4 Easy Steps

1. Install Module

Make sure estate_base and purchase are installed, then activate this module.

2. Create BOQs

Create BOQ sheets for project towers, assign work phases, and enter estimated material lines.

3. Submit Requests

Site engineers request materials against BOQ caps; managers review and approve requests.

4. Generate POs

Click "Create Purchase Order" to automatically send RFQs to material vendors.
